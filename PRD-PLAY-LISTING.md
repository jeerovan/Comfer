# Google Play listing optimization

Optimize Comfer's Play listing with evidence from its current listing, available
performance exports, verified app behavior and free keyword sources. Listing
versions are independent of Android release versions.

- Preserve the observed listing copy and all asset types/locales as version 1 in
  a new `play_listing.db`, with timestamps, source identities and asset hashes.
- Fetch available acquisition, install, rating and review evidence. Preserve
  report generations, metric definitions, date windows and breakdowns; missing
  access/data is not zero. Keep clicks, acquisitions and installs distinct.
- Research free keyword sources and record actual signals separately from
  hypotheses. Select a focused English iteration from the observed audience.
- Prepare version 2 title (30 characters), short description (80), full
  description (4,000), a 1024×500 feature graphic and genuine app screenshots.
  Provide editable sources and upload files. Do not generate a video.
  The first candidate targets the existing en-US listing, with five phone,
  five portrait-tablet and five landscape-tablet images. Keep the existing icon
  and video URL. Additional listing locales require their own later review.
- Verify all advertised capabilities against release 58 and avoid unsupported
  privacy, speed, battery, pricing or ranking claims. Preserve app/user data.
- Keep version 2's preparation, publication and performance evaluation states
  distinct. Record an actual activation date only after publication is verified.
  Provide repeatable collection and like-for-like comparison for later iterations.
- Create and validate a discoverable `play-store-assets` skill covering Google
  Play research, listing media, versioned evidence and evaluation. Keep it
  independent of Apple's app-store-assets skill and of Comfer-specific secrets.

Acceptance requires the saved baseline, real available performance data,
validated reviewable version 2 artifacts, tested collection/evaluation tooling,
and the installed skill. Live publication is a separate concrete action after
the prepared candidate is reviewable; no experiment or schedule is implied.
